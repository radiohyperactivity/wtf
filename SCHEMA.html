WtF Schema

Entity-relationship diagram in crow's foot notation, of db/migrations/0001_schema.sql as of 2 November 2026. See SCHEMA.md.

  • class.attributes holds every attribute the class has, its own and those it inherits, by name, in JSON; item.values and stock.values hold the values by attribute name, and defaulted the names of those that follow the class's default. The accessors check them; there are no triggers.
  • class.parent and location.parent form trees; the move functions refuse cycles. Sibling names are unique, letter case ignored.
  • item.code, location.code and stock.code are label codes from one counter, meta.code, so they are unique across all three tables; the API shows them in Crockford Base32.
  • meta is one row: the label code counter, and the versions of the class tree and the location tree.
  • photo on item and stock, and the values of attachment attributes, are the SHA-256 of a file's content, as hex; the bytes live in wtfd's files directory, named by it, once however often they are used.
  • item.unit is a label for its stock amounts (NULL: pieces); item.search is its search text, written by the accessors.
  • One database is one installation: there are no tenants.
